# Concept: "3 AM, Chat Never Sleeps" (first brand film, 45–60 s)

> A proposal. The product functions and pain points below are placeholders based on common streamer
> problems. Replace them with Chobbot's real features (see `NEEDS.md`) and the scenes keep their shape.

## The idea in one paragraph

One streamer, alone at 3 AM, drowning in their own chat. The film starts in the dark room, lit only by
monitors, and turns each pain point into a **physical event**: chat literally floods the room, spam
cracks the screen glass, a raid hits like a storm against the window, a missed donation falls into the
dark. At the drop, **Chobbot** arrives, and every chaos the song described gets sorted, tagged, caught
or answered, on the beat. The camera then pulls back through the monitor into a city where every window
is another stream, calmer now, and lands on the logo.

## Characters

- **The streamer.** Hoodie, headset, seen in silhouette and close details: hands, the headset, light
  in the eyes. We never see a full face, so any streamer can project themselves into the role. This
  also avoids the uncanny-face problem the pdoom bible warns about.
- **Chobbot.** Not a cartoon mascot with a face. It's a **small point of brand-coloured light that
  drags a line** (pdoom's "spark" idea, made ours): it moves through the chat, and everything it
  touches gets sorted. If the brand has a mascot, it can become a voxel toy built from it, the way
  Venmar and Quest are built from 32×32 sprites.

## Visual rules

- One accent colour (the brand's) is the only thing that glows. Everything else is ink, graphite, bone
  and screen-white.
- Pain points are **cold and broken**: glitch, crack, desaturated. Solutions are **warm and clean**:
  bloom, order, and smooth springs.
- No real platform UIs or logos; chat and streams are our own generic design.
- On-screen type carries the message without sound: each pain point gets 2–4 words, set in the brand's
  font and synced to the lyric.

## Song brief (write the track to this)

- **Length:** 50–60 s for the hero film, or a 2-minute song we cut down.
- **Tempo:** 120–140 BPM, 4/4. A clear kick and snare, because the cuts and slams ride on them.
- **Structure:** intro 4 bars → verse 8 → pre 4 → pre 4 → **1-bar break with real silence** →
  **drop** 8 → chorus 8 → outro 4.
- **Lyrics:** name the pain points in plain words in the verse and pre-chorus (each line ≈ one
  scene). The drop or chorus line is the brand promise and includes the name "Chobbot".
- **Mix:** a clear lead vocal, little reverb on the words, so the word alignment is accurate.
  Exporting **stems** (vocal + instrumental) from Suno/Udio helps the alignment a lot.
- **Vibe options:** (a) dark melodic phonk/trap, night-drive mood; (b) synth-pop, bright and catchy
  for a broader brand; (c) lo-fi into a hard drop, which matches the "chaos → calm" story best.
  Recommendation: (c).
