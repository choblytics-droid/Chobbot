# Ferrugem Na Fenda x Cowbell Infection — treatment

**One line:** two pixel creatures, Venmar (singer A, cyan dragon-cat) and Quest (singer B, orange fox),
turn a diss track into a night in a rusted city split by a glowing rift. "Ferrugem na fenda" means
"rust in the rift".

**Look:** voxel-extruded pixel art in a rain-soaked night city. The grade is a teal/orange split owned
by the two singers (Venmar's verses grade cool, Quest's warm). Rust and navy tie it together, and the
infection green and cowbell gold are rare accents. The phonk cowbell drives flashes, glitches and
lightning; kicks drive camera punch-ins and shake.

**"Him":** the lyrics' antagonist is never a real character. He's a faceless hooded shadow with red eyes:
building the fortress, blasting rumours, playing the victim, trembling, and finally the head the whole
story happens inside.

**Type voices:** Archivo slams (current word huge), JRPG dialogue boxes with pixel portraits (Quest),
elegant Cormorant italic (the throne, "cozy in your head"), found-footage mono captions, and pixel-font
game HUDs.
